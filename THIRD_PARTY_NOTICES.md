## libx86emu

- Repository: https://github.com/wfeldt/libx86emu
- Pinned commit: `ce81129c57fdfbf690bebc210a8db97d926cc5e7`
- Location: `third_party/libx86emu` (source snapshot, with UWP portability patches).
- License: permissive BSD-style; retain its copyright and permission notices.
  The exact text is in `third_party/libx86emu/LICENSE` and is included in the
  UWP package as `ThirdParty/libx86emu-LICENSE.txt`.
- Original file hashes and our changes are recorded in `UPSTREAM.sha256.json`
  and `XCDM-INTEGRATION.md` in that directory.

This is an integer x86 interpreter, not a complete Windows or gaming runtime.
The PE32 Win32 fixture is self-authored and generated by
`samples/make_x86_win32.py`; it contains no third-party executable code.
